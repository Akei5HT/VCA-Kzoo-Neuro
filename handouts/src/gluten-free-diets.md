A strict **gluten-free diet** may help some dogs with paroxysmal dyskinesia (movement disorder), particularly **Border Terriers**. The diet must be fed **exclusively** — including treats and chews — for at least 3 months to judge whether it helps.

> **Check the form!** Some products are gluten-free only as **dry** or only as **canned**. The other form may contain gluten.
