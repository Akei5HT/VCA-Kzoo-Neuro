Brain Tumor (Glioma) Clinical Trials

Some universities offer clinical trials for dogs with glioma (glial cell brain tumors). Many trials cover most or all treatment costs. Eligibility usually requires an MRI consistent with glioma, recent blood work and chest X-rays, and no other major illness.

Please note: trial listings change often. This list was last checked in January 2023 — contact each program directly for current studies. We are happy to send your pet's MRI images and records.

Purdue University

Canine glioma trial — surgery plus a virus therapy placed in the tumor cavity. The study covers surgery, post-operative care, monitoring and repeat MRIs at 1, 3 and 6 months. vet.purdue.edu/ctr/clinical-research/canine.php

University of Minnesota — Dr. Liz Pluhar

Canine brain tumor clinical trials program. Your veterinarian can apply online, or you may contact Dr. Pluhar directly; she will review the MRI to decide eligibility.

  • caninegliomatrial.com
  • 612-625-1162 · pluha006@umn.edu
  • UMN Neurology appointments: 612-626-8387

CANINE Trial — UAB, Auburn, Mississippi State, Georgia

Multicenter immunotherapy study for malignant brain tumors. Treatment (surgery, virus therapy ± checkpoint inhibitor, imaging, hospitalization and scheduled rechecks) is paid for by the study.

  • uab.edu/medicine/caninetrial
  • vetmed.msstate.edu — Glial Cell Tumor Studies

Virginia Tech (Virginia-Maryland CVM)

Inclusion: dogs 3–45 kg, mild to moderate brain signs, a single MRI mass consistent with glioma, and no other significant disease. Submit the online Neurology Clinical Trial Consultation Form. vetmed.vt.edu/clinical-trials/canine-gliomas.html

How to get started

  1. Ask us for a copy of your pet's MRI images (DICOM) and report.
  2. Contact the program, or ask us to contact them for you.
  3. The trial team reviews the images and lets you know whether your pet may qualify.
