During your pet's care, a team of highly trained professionals works together. Here are the roles you may meet.

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Client Service Representative (CSR)** | Usually your first point of contact. Welcomes you and your pet, helps with paperwork and billing, and coordinates with the medical team to make your visit smooth. |
| **Veterinary Assistant** | Gathers your pet's history, takes vital signs, assists during the exam, and helps with treatments, lab tests and imaging under the supervision of a veterinarian or credentialed technician. |
| **Veterinary Technician Student** | Enrolled in a 2–4 year AVMA-accredited program. Performs assistant duties and trains in hands-on skills under close supervision. |
| **Credentialed Veterinary Technician** | Has passed a national board exam. Under a veterinarian's supervision, provides much of your pet's hands-on care — anesthesia, nursing, treatments and monitoring. |
| **Veterinary Technician Specialist (VTS)** | A credentialed technician with at least 3 additional years of specialty training who has passed a specialty certification exam. |
| **Veterinary Student** | In a 4-year program training to become a veterinarian. |
| **Rotating Intern** | A veterinarian completing a 1-year internship to build clinical skills. |
| **Neurology Intern** | A veterinarian who has completed a rotating internship and is doing an additional year of neurology-focused training. |
| **Neurology Resident** | A veterinarian in a 3-year training program to become a board-certified neurologist; always supervised. |
| **Board-Certified Neurologist** | Has completed extensive post-graduate training (typically about 9 years after college) and passed rigorous board examinations in internal medicine and neurology (Diplomate, ACVIM — Neurology). |

Questions about your pet's care? Any member of our team can help you or connect you with the right person.
